# 📈 BCG X — Campaign Performance Analysis

**Type:** Forage Introduction to Data for Decision Makers Job Simulation · **Completed:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** Independent learning simulation; not employment with BCG X.

## Business question
How do campaign and marketing-channel results compare for **new customers**?

## Analysis
I reviewed an Excel dataset covering Campaign A and B across Email, Instagram and Web Banner, built a PivotTable, filtered the customer-type slicer to **New**, and developed a chart and recommendation slide.

The screenshot-supported channel averages were approximately:

| Channel | Average sale value |
|---|---:|
| Email | $44.96 |
| Instagram | $20.38 |
| Web Banner | $14.21 |

The email-channel averages for Campaign A and B were approximately **$45.20** and **$44.59**, respectively. My presentation recommended prioritising Campaign A via Email based on the new-customer comparison.

## Business interpretation
Email showed the highest **average sale value** in the displayed segment. This is **not proof of highest ROI or lowest acquisition cost**; those require campaign spending, margins and conversion metrics.
